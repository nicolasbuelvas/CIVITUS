# Privacy Policy for CIVITUS

Last updated: September 2026

CIVITUS ("we", "our", or "the game") is an offline-first procedural space survival and exploration video game developed by Nicolas Buelvas. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected when you download, install, and play CIVITUS via the Samsung Galaxy Store or other platforms.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect
CIVITUS is designed with privacy-first and offline-first principles. We do NOT collect:
- Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, address, phone number, or contacts.
- Real-time physical location data or GPS coordinates.
- Audio, microphone, camera, or biometric data.

## 2. Information Handled for Game Services

### A. In-App Purchases (RevenueCat & Samsung In-App Purchase)
When you purchase optional digital licenses (such as Ad Suppression, Planet Editor, or Full Game Access):
- Purchases are processed directly and securely through the Samsung Galaxy Store In-App Purchase system and RevenueCat.
- We never receive, store, or have access to your credit card details, bank information, or payment credentials.
- Purchase entitlements and license tokens are stored locally on your device (`user://license.cfg`) to guarantee offline access.

### B. Advertising (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob)
- CIVITUS offers voluntary rewarded transmissions and cadence interstitials between planetary expeditions.
- Third-party ad providers may collect non-sensitive device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID or Android ID) according to their own privacy policies to serve contextual or personalized advertisements.
- Players can completely eliminate third-party advertisements at any time by acquiring the "No Ads" license in the in-game store.

### C. Local Game Progression
- All planet generation seeds, inventory, Luna Points, unlocked suits, and mission progress are stored strictly on your local device storage.
- Uninstalling the application will remove local save files unless backed up by your system.

## 3. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA / GDPR-K)
CIVITUS does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. All gameplay systems and procedural algorithms operate locally without collecting personal data.

## 4. Security & Offline Reliability
The game is engineered to function fully without an active internet connection. No network access is required to explore worlds, craft items, pilot spacecraft, or access unlocked content.
